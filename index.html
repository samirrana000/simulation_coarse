Chemical Network & Binding Kinetics

What this is: a 4-state kinetic model of binding (Bulk → Encounter → Intermediate → Bound). MD runs in picoseconds; binding takes microseconds. This model bridges the gap — it watches your live 3D ligand pose, classifies it into S0–S3, and derives rates from the barriers (Kramers / detailed balance). The circles show residence probability; the glow marks the state your ligand is in right now.

Theory: states, rates & detailed balance
  • S0 Bulk Solvated: Unbound ligand freely diffusing in solvent.
  • S1 Encounter Complex: Non-specific surface electrostatic attraction.
  • S2 Intermediate: Pocket vestibule entrance & partial dehydration.
  • S3 Native Bound: Stereospecific pocket lock & hydrogen bonded pose.

Rate calculation: Kramers TST kij = ν0 exp(−ΔG‡ij / kBT) with detailed balance πi Kij = πj Kji. Barriers are heuristic defaults (2.5/3.8/4.5 kcal/mol) — enable Funnel bias to derive them from your PMF.
