Energy model

Total potential: U = Σpeptide ½kb(r−r0)2 + Σangles ½kθ(θ−θ0)2 + Σcontacts ½γH(Rc−r0)(r−r0)2 + Σnon-native ε[(re/r)12−2(re/r)6+1], r<re

Langevin equation: m·r̈ = −∇U − mζṙ + √(2mζkBT)·ξ(t), integrated with the BAOAB scheme (ξ = unit Gaussian white noise).

Protein–ligand binding: U = Σ 4ε[(σ/r)¹²−(σ/r)&sup6;] (cross LJ, ε/σ from residue×element tables) + Σ 332·q&sub1;q&sub2;/(εr·r), εr = 4+76·tanh(r/8) (screened electrostatics) + Σ −εHB·exp(−(r−3.2)²/2·0.6²) (H-bonds, polar donor/acceptor) + Σ ΔGt·(1−e−n/3) (EEF1-lite desolvation, n = protein density) + Σ ½γlig(r−r0)² (holo pose springs, r0 ≤ 6 Å)
